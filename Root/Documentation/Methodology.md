# Methodology

## Overview

This research employs a hybrid analytical approach combining a technical examination of AI systems with a theoretical exploration of alternative cognitive frameworks. My methodology consists of four interconnected components:

## Conceptual Analysis

I begin by analyzing how large language models process information, focusing mainly on the mathematical foundations of vector space operations in transformer architectures. This analysis involves deconstructing the attention mechanisms, embedding processes, and dimensionality operations that form the computational basis of these systems.

## Theoretical Modeling

Building on this technical foundation, I develop theoretical models that extend our understanding of vector space cognition beyond current implementations. These models include the Hyperdimensional Communication System (HCS) framework and the proposed geometric intelligence paradigm. Each model is constructed to explain observed AI capabilities and predict potential emergent properties.

## Visual Representation

I employ diagrammatic representations of complex vector space operations to bridge the gap between abstract mathematical concepts and intuitive understanding. These visualizations are analytical tools and communication devices, allowing me to explore multidimensional concepts through accessible 2D representations.

## Comparative Framework

Throughout the research, I maintain a comparative lens between human and machine cognitive architectures. This comparison is not evaluative but exploratory, seeking to understand fundamental differences in information processing, representation, and reasoning without privileging either approach.

The methodology deliberately spans technical, philosophical, and cognitive domains, reflecting the interdisciplinary nature of the research question. By simultaneously approaching AI cognition from multiple perspectives, I aim to develop a more comprehensive understanding of alternative intelligence frameworks than is possible through any single disciplinary approach.
