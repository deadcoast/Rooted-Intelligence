>[!question] [1]  [[1.pdf|Firth, J. R. (1957). _A synopsis of linguistic theory, 1930-1955_. In Studies in Linguistic Analysis (pp. 1-32). Basil Blackwell.)]]
>> [!note] 
>> Relevance to ‘Rooted Intelligence’: This work establishes the foundational linguistic principle (the distributional hypothesis) that meaning is derived from context (“You shall know a word by the company it keeps”). This relates to the paper’s core concept of representing meaning within a “hyperdimensional semantic space” where relationships are derived from data patterns instead of fixed symbolic definitions. It provides the linguistic precursor to the computational vector space models described.
